# Valley life — interface and map concept targets

Nine concept images for a desktop observational social simulation. Images are AI-generated visual targets, not verified geographic models or final interface specifications. No synced sources were edited. Generated rasters were 1672 × 941; the final PNGs are resampled exports at exactly 1920 × 1080.

Owner-supplied concept art (2026-10-22): visual targets for the living map, not evidence of geography or floor plans. Five of the nine images are saved here as concept-1…5.webp; the rest were shared in chat.

## Shared build targets

- Export canvas: 1920 × 1080, 16:9.
- Type: Segoe UI / SF Pro style system sans-serif. Use live text in the product.
- Panels: 8–12px radius, thin borders, soft shadows, generous padding.
- Light map: ground #ecebe6; buildings #d9d7cf. Dark map: ground #141413; buildings #262624.
- Activity colors: Home #2a78d6; Class #eb6834; Work #1baf7a; Church #eda100; Campus #e87ba4; Gym #008300; Get-together #4a3aa7; Date #e34948.
- Only buildings used by people rise in 3D. Other buildings remain flat footprints. Dots must be anchored to real floor and room positions.
- Building footprints, heights, resident counts, and dot totals need data validation in the implemented game; concept renders are not evidence of spatial accuracy.

## Copy decisions

Generic names replace branded references: Hillside Halls and Citrus Table. The Right now categories in the supplied brief total 235; adding Other 4 reconciles the 239 occasions. The 520 people are shown separately from the stadium crowd. The person card preserves the supplied married/dating and Mia examples, although these need a narrative consistency decision before shipping. Dinner = 8, etc. are illustrative occasion counts.
