# Workstream: Provenance and Trace

> Spec written for the iteration 6 builders. Status and where the code is: docs/HANDOFF.md. Shared rules: docs/workstreams/README.md. Contract: docs/EVOLUTION.md.

Provenance and Trace: make the world's history traceable. FIRST: two previous attempts were cut off by usage limits and left substantial work on branch wip/provenance (8 WIP commits: provenance helpers in ucsim/engine/provenance.py, decision vocabularies, romance/plans/coping/economy/housing/faith/lifecourse/emotion instrumentation, the trace engine and chains in ucsim/analysis/trace.py, routes_trace.py, tests). Start with "git merge --no-edit wip/provenance", check what's done against your scope below (run the tests and scripts/provenance_coverage.py), fix what isn't right, and finish the rest (in particular the Trace and Chains workbench tools and kit/DecisionCard.tsx if missing). Measured before this iteration: only 27% of events had a recorded cause (new friendships, asking out, gatherings, job finds, illness, faith challenges never; breakups 4%), and causes were always a single parent event. You instrument the decision sites outside the time/occasions builder's domain, add multi-cause links, and build the trace engine, its API and two workbench tools — this is the heart of what the owner asked for: "a way for me to select and see causal events, the chain reaction of things, at micro and macro level — not auto-generated neat things".
1. Measure first: scripts/provenance_coverage.py runs a small world ~60 days and reports, per event type, the share with a parent cause, a decision, or typed causes; report before/after (goal: ≥ 90% of events with salience ≥ 0.5, and most others).
2. Instrument (record decisions with their real terms; link events): romance.py _asks (asking out, accepting), weekly couple dynamics (DTR, engagement, breakups — who ended it and why), _end, _weddings (another builder edits plan_day/evening/_run_dates; a third edits _kiss/_sex/_ncmo/_confessions — do not edit those); network.py friendship transitions (new_friend, close_friend, friendship_faded: causes = where they met (origin_ctx/origin_place, including occasions), who introduced them, the context where most of their time together accrued, shared experiences); economy.py (job search, found, quit, lost: factors such as financial stress, coping, intentions); coping.py (strategy choice, start/end); plans.py (intention formation and abandonment with reasons); housing.py (moves and choice of place: rent, friends nearby, culture fit); faith.py (attendance changes vs usual, crisis start and outcome, renewal); lifecourse.py (mission call, early return, dropout, leaving the area) where reasons exist; emotion.py (depressive episode start: contributing terms). Declare every decision kind and factor in config/decisions/20_*.yaml with plain words.
3. Ties: in Encounters.apply (you own apply(); the occasions builder edits run()), track per tie the context where most of their time together happened (e.g. top context + hours and a runner-up as EdgeStore columns — see engine/state.py), and when ctx == CTX_ID["occasion"] report scene counts with sim.book.note_scene(place_ids, talks=..., new_ties=..., conflicts=...) and keep origin_ctx/origin_place (the occasion id) on first meetings.
4. Trace engine ucsim/analysis/trace.py: given a ref (event, decision, occasion, person, tie "lo-hi", var@person, place) build a causal graph backward (what led to it) and forward (what it led to) to a depth, merging event parents, typed causes, decisions and their factor refs, occasions (who came, who met whom), ties (where they met, introducer), feeling decompositions (analysis/explain.explain_state, with the source events of decaying effects) and memories. Nodes: day/block/minute, people involved, one plain line (use event 'story' templates), kind, weight. Links: kind (caused, decided_by, factor, attended, met_at, introduced, contributed, led_to), weight, words. Be honest: decomposition terms and factors "contributed" with a weight; cap node counts and say what was cut.
   Population view chains(sim, since, until, filter): flows between event types through cause links and decisions (A → B counts with example instances), and from decision factors to outcomes ("of 340 declined invitations, 41% had tiredness as the top reason"), for a cohort and window.
5. Endpoints (ucsim/api/routes_trace.py): GET /api/trace?u=&ref=&dir=back|fwd|both&depth=, GET /api/trace/chains?u=&since=&until=&filter=(json)&root=(event type), GET /api/decision/{id}?u=, GET /api/person/{i}/decisions?u=&since=.
6. Workbench tools (web/src/bench/tools/): Trace.tsx — the focused item's causes and consequences laid out left→right in time with one horizontal lane per person (lane labels are names, clickable), nodes as small shapes by kind (event, decision, occasion, state), links as curves with words on hover; click a node to focus it or show more causes; filter by kind; depth control; decisions open a factor card; readable in light and dark. Chains.tsx — population chain reactions as a left→right flow (SVG or ECharts' sankey, already a dependency); click a flow to list example instances (each opens in Trace). web/src/kit/DecisionCard.tsx — a decision's factors as diverging bars with their words ('close to the organizer', 'tired'), constraints as 'couldn't' badges, the outcome and its probability. Use useBench for selection; tools register by exporting tool. If the workbench shell isn't in your worktree, build against registry.ts types and check with npm run build.
Tests: tests/test_provenance.py (coverage thresholds on a small world; every factor declared; a friendship's causes point at where they met), tests/test_trace.py (graph shapes, depth, caps, chains).
