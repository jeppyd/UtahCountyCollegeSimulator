# Workstream: The LDS Sexuality Survey

> Spec written for the iteration 6 builders. Status and where the code is: docs/HANDOFF.md. Shared rules: docs/workstreams/README.md. Contract: docs/EVOLUTION.md.

incorporate the owner's fifth survey, "The LDS Sexuality Survey" (June 2025; 1,641 respondents; 154 columns), into the model. The raw file is /home/user/UtahCountyCollegeSimulator/data/raw/lds-sexuality_2025-06-29.xlsx (gitignored; real respondents). PRIVACY IS PARAMOUNT: read it in place (never copy it into your worktree), never commit or print respondent rows, never read free-text columns — City, College, every "Other - Text", the fantasy / favorite activity / wildest place / "anything else to share" questions, the birth-control free text, and the extra sheets Fantasy, Favorites, Location, Dropdowns (skip those sheets entirely). Commit only aggregates where every cell has ≥ 10 respondents (suppress, pool or back off smaller cells), following docs/DATA_SOURCES.md's privacy boundary exactly as the other four surveys do (see ucsim/ingest/priors.py, surveys.py, survey_spec.py, K_MIN). The other four surveys' raw CSV exports are now in data/raw/ too, but their committed aggregates must NOT change (re-reading the CSVs does not reproduce them exactly), so build this survey's aggregates incrementally: a module ucsim/ingest/sexuality.py (runnable as python -m ucsim.ingest.sexuality) that reads the xlsx with openpyxl (add openpyxl to an optional dependency group such as 'ingest' in pyproject.toml), harmonizes columns (spec entries in survey_spec.py, aliased like the others), restricts priors to the model's in-scope population (ages 18–35; report how many respondents that keeps and their make-up), and writes only its own sections into ucsim/priors/survey_priors.json (e.g. blocks.lds_sexuality, new targets.*, tables.*, meta.sources) without changing other surveys' sections.
Columns (header text): Gender, Age, Relationship Status (Single / In a relationship / Engaged / Married), Utah?, Sexuality (Straight / Bisexual / Gay / Asexual / Pansexual / Other), Politics, Membership, Activity (Active / Less Active / Inactive), Mission, Temple, Drive (Very low…Very high); "When - …" items (the relationship stage at which each of ~19 physical activities is acceptable: Casual Dating (On a date) / Committed Relationship / Engagement / Marriage / Never); "How many premaritally? …" counts; "Did you and your spouse do this? …" (married: done with their future spouse before marriage); moral-wrongness-in-marriage items and "do you and your spouse ever do this" items; married-life items (desire difference, frequency, satisfaction with sex life and with marriage, scheduling, rough transition after marriage, talking about sex with a friend/parent/sibling); "Select the period(s)…" (multi-select life periods for porn images/videos/masturbation) and current frequencies; "addressed with a bishop" (Pornography / Masturbation); "How much were you taught about sex growing up?"; "How many… people kissed, NCMO partners, romantic relationships, age at first relationship, age at first kiss, years married"; attitude items (porn morally wrong; single masturbation wrong; feelings negatively impacted by church teachings; would enjoy a same-sex experience; uncomfortable talking about sex; sex is a good thing; a wife's duty…); five dating "dealbreaker" items (read the full header strings).
1. Map to the model (read config/variables/*.yaml for what exists: porn_use, porn_incongruence, chastity_commitment, premarital_sex_current, n_sex_partners, kissing/NCMO counts, orientation, lgbtq_tension, relationship_satisfaction…). Use the survey to (a) ground existing variables' starting distributions where it is the better source (conditioned on sex, age band, relationship status, activity as sample sizes allow; population/synth.py is yours to edit for this), (b) add a few new person variables that change behavior, e.g. sex_drive, intimacy_boundary (the stage at which increasingly intimate tiers are acceptable — a compact representation: per tier or a boundary score), sex_ed_home, sexual_shame (feelings negatively affected by teachings), and for married people sexual_satisfaction and desire_gap. Give these and the existing sexual-behavior variables flags: [sensitive] in their YAML (the interview builder excludes sensitive fields from dossiers).
2. Mechanism — put it in a NEW system module ucsim/engine/systems/intimacy.py registered in sim.py (call it from the existing schedule, e.g. after romance.evening and in the weekly phase), keeping edits to romance.py minimal and limited to _kiss, _sex, _ncmo, _confessions (other builders edit other romance functions; to end a relationship call the existing romance._end(...) rather than editing it): physical intimacy escalation in couples — as a relationship moves through stages (dating → exclusive → engaged → married) couples move along the intimacy ladder at a pace set by both partners' boundaries, drive, chastity commitment and opportunity (time alone); crossing one's own boundary raises guilt (appraisal already scales events by what people care about), may lead to talking to a bishop (the survey's 'addressed with a bishop' shares), and mismatched boundaries add conflict; married couples' sexual satisfaction and desire gap feed relationship_satisfaction modestly. Record decisions (config/decisions/40_intimacy.yaml) and events (config/events/40_intimacy.yaml) with causes. Tasteful plain labels in everything user-facing ('became more physically intimate', 'crossed a line they had set for themselves', 'talked to their bishop'). Evidence-labeled, calibrated so existing kissing/NCMO/premarital-sex validation rows don't get worse (VALIDATION.md notes kissing counts already run high); use the new survey's kissing/NCMO/relationship counts by age, sex and activity as targets, some held out.
2b. Dealbreakers (approved by the owner): use the five dating "dealbreaker" items (read their full header strings). Represent each person's dealbreakers (the share agreeing, conditioned on sex/activity/mission as sample sizes allow); a partner's relevant past or practice (e.g. their sexual history, porn use, past choices the items name — map each item to model variables) becomes known to the other through the relationship's own history (the tie's disclosure/confiding builds over time; a confession or a crossed line can reveal it), and finding out can end the relationship (a decision dealbreaker_reaction with factors: how strongly they hold the dealbreaker, closeness and commitment, how long together, the partner's honesty) or not; record events ('found out something that was a dealbreaker', 'decided it wasn't a dealbreaker after all') with causes pointing at the disclosure.
3. Validation: rows in ucsim/config/validation.json and scripts/validate.py (people kissed, NCMO partners, number of relationships, age at first kiss and first relationship by sex/activity; premarital intercourse share by activity; porn use frequency by sex and activity; share who addressed porn/masturbation with a bishop; married sexual and marital satisfaction), with diagnostic/held-out flags as appropriate; run validation at scale 0.15 before/after and report.
4. Docs and tests: a subsection in docs/DATA_SOURCES.md (what was used, sample size and make-up, biases: self-selected LDStats audience, broad age range, mostly Utah), data/README.md (the new raw file name), tests/test_sexuality_priors.py (no committed cell < 10, no long arrays, no free text, the builder refuses to run without the raw file and leaves other sections untouched), tests/test_intimacy.py (escalation respects boundaries on average; determinism).
