# Workstream: Time, occasions and social actions (option B)

> Spec written for the iteration 6 builders. Status and where the code is: docs/HANDOFF.md. Shared rules: docs/workstreams/README.md. Contract: docs/EVOLUTION.md.

Time and occasions (the owner's chosen "option B"): the four-block day, occasions with invitations and attendance (scenes), shifts with clock times, feasibility with travel, and user social actions. The owner chose: planned things (dinners, dates, clubs) get start times and lengths; people check whether they can make it given commitments, travel and how late they'd be; a ride changes whether they can get there; social actions: invite, introduce, offer a ride, start a study group or a recurring dinner — first as a world editor (arrange the opportunity; people still decide), plus acting AS a person, limited to whom they know and their time and money. Example the owner gave: moving a dinner later lets a working student attend and meet a study partner, while hosting becomes a burden for the organizer; whether the dinner survives must emerge from the simulation.

1. Four blocks (ucsim/engine/clock.py exists): Simulation.PHASES gets block3 at 20:00 (clock.BLOCK_HOUR); Activity.choose, encounters, romance's evening, groups, mind.observe_block, routine (28 weekly slots), plans, culture, recorder and history work with 4 blocks. Replace "b == 2"-style evening checks deliberately: FHE Monday ~7 pm (evening block), ward activities weeknights ~7 pm, dances Fri/Sat ~9 pm (late block), clubs/teams/institute in afternoon or evening blocks with realistic start times, dates spanning evening→late. Encounter rates and contact durations per block scale with block length (clock.LENGTH_H vs the legacy 5-hour evening) so someone who stays in one ambient context all evening accumulates about the same hours as before. Day-replay frames: 4 blocks + night — update /api/positions/day in ucsim/api/server.py and DAY_LABELS/frame count in web/src/views/MapView.tsx (the only edits you make to those two files).
2. Occasions system ucsim/engine/systems/occasions.py, registered in sim.py, built on sim.book (ledger.OccasionBook):
 - It replaces Activity._gatherings: hosts decide (decision host_occasion), choose a kind (gathering / dinner / game night / party…, by day of week, apartment culture, personality) and a start time, plan it (sometimes days ahead for weekend parties), invite friends (closeness-weighted; roommates and neighbors in person, others by text; messaging comes in a later wave — leave a clear hook like occasions.deliver(inv_ids, channel)), invitees notice (in person at once; texts with a chance that rises with phone use and falls when busy; record seen_day), decide (attend_occasion — factors in config/decisions/00_core.yaml, adjust/extend in your own config/decisions/10_occasions.yaml; constraints conflict and cant_get_there), and attend in the blocks the occasion overlaps (ctx = CTX_ID["occasion"], cid = occasion id; location from the host's unit or the venue). Some who said yes don't come (record why) and some come late. Household members who are home join as walk-ins.
 - Open occasions (open: true): attendance from the kind's audience/base/interest/capacity fields (documented at the top of config/occasions/00_core.yaml), with travel and conflicts — generic, because the calendar builder is adding kinds like football games and devotionals with audiences in parallel and only plans them.
 - Dates become occasions (kind date, at a venue, with a start time; both attend unless something prevents it; edit only romance.py's plan_day / evening / _run_dates as needed — other builders edit other romance functions). Dances become open occasions at dance venues.
 - Traditions: a successful occasion can become a weekly series (a host decision); a series continues if a resident takes it over when the host moves out, or ends. Emit events (config/events/10_occasions.yaml: e.g. tradition_started, tradition_passed_on, tradition_ended) with causes.
 - Encounters: pass occasion contexts through Encounters.run (rate per occasion kind × overlap share). Another builder owns Encounters.apply and adds scene counting there (book.note_scene; origin_ctx/origin_place for first meetings) — do not change apply() beyond what's needed to pass ctx/place, and keep its signature.
 - social_events_month (validated against ProvoStats) counts attended social occasions plus ward activities/FHE/clubs as before; keep its meaning.
 - Helpers used by UI and actions: free_window(i, day), can_make(people, occ) → (feasible, minutes late, travel minutes, mode, reason), agenda(i, day) (a person's day: blocks, commitments, occasions with times, travel).
3. Shifts with clock times: per-weekday shift start/end minutes in P.internal (students: afternoons/evenings/weekends; others: daytime), block occupancy derived from them; job_found/lost/quit keep working; a hook for employer flexibility (institutions come later): occasions.shift_conflict(i, occ).
4. Travel: use sim.travel.trip(...) (ucsim/geo/travel.py; another builder is replacing its internals with real street and transit matrices — don't edit it). An offered ride passes ride=True for that trip.
5. Social actions: intervention atoms {"target_type": "social", "action": invite|series|reschedule|introduce|ride|reach_out|job_offer, ...} in ucsim/engine/interventions.py (lowered to occasions/invitations/ties; origin "user"; cause kind "action"), validated with plain-language refusal reasons; "as": <person id> means acting as that person (only people they know, only when they're free, only what they can afford). Endpoints in ucsim/api/routes_act.py: POST /api/act/check (preview for each person involved: free? travel? late? why not) and POST /api/act (apply to the What-if world, creating it by forking the Original if none exists, the way /api/fork does). Add the actions to the What-if compiler's lever catalog (ucsim/llm/levers.py, and plan.py's schema/validator if needed) so requests like "Maya invites her roommates to dinner at 7:30 on Sunday" compile.
6. Activity.explain_choice(i, b): a person's free-time option utilities now, broken into terms (computed on demand; labeled as computed now).
7. Endpoints (ucsim/api/routes_occasions.py): GET /api/occasion/{id}?u= (the scene: when, where, organizer, invitations with channel/seen/response and the decision's factors, who came and when, how they got there, who met whom there, events there), GET /api/occasions?u=&day=&kind=&person=&place=, GET /api/person/{i}/day?u=&day= (agenda), GET /api/person/{i}/choice?u=&block= (explain_choice).
8. Realism: use scripts/validate.py (check --help) at scale 0.15 to compare key rows before/after (social events per month, dating frequency, friends, clustering, hours to friendship); adjust gathering/encounter parameters modestly to stay close and report a before/after table. Runtime budget: at most +30% per simulated day.
Tests: tests/test_clock.py, tests/test_occasions.py, tests/test_social_actions.py (feasibility; a working student who can make 7:30 but not 6:00; a ride making an occasion reachable; as-person refusals; paired-world determinism with and without an action).
