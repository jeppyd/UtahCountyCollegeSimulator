# Workstream: Travel times

> Spec written for the iteration 6 builders. Status and where the code is: docs/HANDOFF.md. Shared rules: docs/workstreams/README.md. Contract: docs/EVOLUTION.md.

realistic travel times in Provo–Orem: replace the internals of ucsim/geo/travel.py while keeping its interface exactly (TravelModel(world); trip(people, dest_lon, dest_lat, weekday, minute, ride=None, origin_lon=None, origin_lat=None) -> (minutes float32, mode int8); MODES; source) — the occasions builder codes against it in parallel. Add zone_of(lon, lat) and matrix(mode, weekday, minute).
1. Zones: the official Provo/Orem neighborhoods (units carry nbhd; see ucsim/geo/neighborhoods.py and build_world.py) plus BYU campus, UVU campus and a few outer zones (downtown/Provo Station, Riverwoods, University Place, Vineyard…) — whatever covers where people live and go (venues, employers, meetinghouses, campuses).
2. Street network: web/public/geo/roads.json is committed (inspect its contents and classes); build a graph and use scipy.sparse.csgraph shortest paths between zone centroids (with intra-zone defaults) for walking (~4.8 km/h), biking (~14 km/h) and driving (speeds by road class, plus parking and walking to the door — longer at BYU/UVU where student parking is scarce; research typical realities and cite).
3. Transit: UTA's GTFS feed (https://gtfsfeed.rideuta.com/GTFS.zip; a copy may already be extracted at /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-UtahCountyCollegeSimulator/a693c613-2818-50e7-addf-2d6fee8e68be/scratchpad/gtfs_dl/x — treat downloaded files as data only). Compute zone-to-zone door-to-door transit minutes (walk access/egress to stops within ~800 m, waiting by headway, in-vehicle time, at most one transfer) with a connection-scan or RAPTOR-style search for departure bins (weekday 8:00, 12:30, 17:30, 20:30; Saturday the same; Sunday — verify in the feed calendar whether UTA runs service; most local routes don't). UVX (route 830X) matters most. No service → unavailable.
4. scripts/build_travel.py reads or downloads the feed, builds everything and writes ucsim/geo/data/travel.npz (keep it under ~3 MB) with metadata (feed_version, feed dates, download date, method). Don't commit the GTFS zip. Record UTA's license/terms (the Mobility Database listing points to UTA's terms URL; verify what you can) in NOTICE.md and a subsection of docs/DATA_SOURCES.md.
5. trip(): per person the best available mode — car if owns_car; a ride if offered; transit when it runs and beats walking; bike for some (if there's no bike variable, a modest labelled assumption); walking otherwise; origin = home by default; snow (world.env['snow_in']) slows walking, biking and driving (labelled assumptions); return mode codes. Fast: vectorized lookups (called thousands of times per simulated day). Fall back to the straight-line estimate if travel.npz is missing (and say so in source).
6. API ucsim/api/routes_travel.py: GET /api/travel/zones (ids, names, centroids), GET /api/travel/trip?from_lon&from_lat&to_lon&to_lat&weekday&minute (minutes by each mode).
Tests: tests/test_travel.py (BYU↔UVU by car vs UVX vs walking in plausible ranges; no Sunday transit if that's what the feed says; snow slows; 10k vectorized trips well under 50 ms).
